## Story direction

The desktop combines the finale's cyberskeleton assault, cyberpsychosis, and Sandevistan velocity with the moon promise that anchors David and Lucy's story. The lockscreen intentionally shows the quiet lunar dream rather than reusing the combat wallpaper.
