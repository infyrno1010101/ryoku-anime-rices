# Scott Pilgrim vs. the World — Twin Dragon Threshold

A native Ryoku movie rice built around Sex Bob-Omb's Battle of the Bands against evil exes Kyle and Ken Katayanagi.

## Desktop
- 10-second 1920×1080 H.264 wallpaper at 24 FPS
- Six authentic film cuts: twin dragons, Scott/bass, monster VS clash, twins, sound-yeti finisher, explosive K.O./1-UP
- Diagonal comic-panel wipes, halftone corners, snow/coin particles, bass shockwave rings, combo and score HUD
- Bangers impact lettering, Press Start 2P arcade telemetry, and the public-domain film wordmark
- Top notch bar, bottom autohide cartridge dock, low-gain side-split visualizer
- Yellow/cyan/pink/black fixed palette, animated comic borders, flash focus, crystal-purple key sounds
- Large desktop widgets and calendar disabled so the film battle remains unobstructed

## Separate lockscreen
`scott-pilgrim-subspace-continue` uses the final Ramona/subspace-door sequence rather than battle footage. It includes a seven-ex rail, `CONTINUE?` timer, 1-UP state, portal rings, player authentication, Power of Self-Respect status, and pixel-shatter unlock.

## Apply
```bash
ryoku-hub rice preflight scott-pilgrim-twin-dragon-threshold
ryoku-hub rice apply scott-pilgrim-twin-dragon-threshold
```
Kasane discovers it automatically as a NATIVE rice. Recovery: `ryoku-hub rice apply pre-scott-pilgrim-baseline`.

The baked media requires no installed fonts. Redistributable font files and OFL texts are bundled under `fonts/`. See `CREDITS.md`.
