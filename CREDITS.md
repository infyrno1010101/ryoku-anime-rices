## Fan-made status

Ryoku Anime Rices is an unofficial, noncommercial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any publisher, studio, production committee, creator, distributor, or rights holder associated with the featured series.

All series names, characters, logos, and underlying source artwork remain the property of their respective owners. No ownership of those materials is claimed. The repository maintainer's contribution is limited to desktop configuration, palettes, animation treatments, layout design, documentation, previews, and supporting scripts.

If you are a rights holder and want attributed media corrected or removed, please open a GitHub issue.
